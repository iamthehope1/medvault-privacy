# Privacy Policy – MedVault

**Effective Date:** May 25, 2026

MedVault ("the App") is a personal project created by **Jassim Al Marzouqi**.

MedVault is a privacy-focused clinical reference and medical knowledge management application built for clinicians, residents, and medical students.

---

## Information We Collect

MedVault is designed around privacy.

The App:

- Does **not require login or registration**
- Does **not collect personal information**
- Does **not sell, rent, share, or transmit user data**

The developer does not collect:

- Names
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Device identifiers
- Location information
- Analytics data
- Advertising identifiers
- Usage tracking information
- Payment information

---

## Local Device Storage

All notes, references, protocols, specialty content, scanned materials, imported documents, and user-created content are stored locally on the user's device.

Your information remains under your control.

MedVault does not upload your content to third-party cloud providers.

---

## Optional Private Sync

MedVault may optionally support synchronization through a user-controlled private server or home network environment.

If enabled:

- synchronization occurs only between systems controlled by the user
- the developer does not access synchronized information
- no third-party cloud storage is used by the developer

---

## Biometric Authentication

MedVault may use device authentication mechanisms such as:

- Face ID
- Touch ID
- Device Passcode / PIN

Authentication is handled entirely through Apple's secure operating system services.

The App does not access, collect, store, or transmit biometric information.

---

## OCR, Scanner, and File Import Features

MedVault may allow users to:

- scan documents
- perform OCR (optical character recognition)
- import Word documents
- import text files

Processing is designed to occur locally on the device whenever supported.

Imported materials remain under user control.

---

## Data Security

MedVault follows a privacy-first, offline-first design philosophy emphasizing local storage and device-level protection.

Users remain responsible for:

- securing their devices
- maintaining backups
- managing device passcodes
- securing any privately managed synchronization servers

---

## Children's Privacy

MedVault is intended for adult healthcare professionals, residents, and medical students.

The App is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.

---

## Third-Party Services

MedVault does not use:

- third-party analytics services
- advertising SDKs
- behavioral tracking systems
- developer-managed cloud storage

---

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.

Updates will be reflected by revising the effective date shown above.
